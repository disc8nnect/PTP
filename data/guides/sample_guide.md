# Pagkain at tubig / Food and water

Kumain ng iba't ibang prutas at gulay araw-araw. Eat a variety of fruits and vegetables every day.

Uminom ng sapat na malinis na tubig. Drink enough clean water.

Hugasan nang mabuti ang prutas at gulay bago kainin. Wash fruits and vegetables well before eating.

# Check-up / Prenatal visits

Pumunta sa mga nakatakdang check-up. Attend your scheduled prenatal check-ups.

Dalhin ang notebook ng check-up at isulat ang mga tanong para sa doktor o midwife. Bring your check-up notebook and write down questions for your doctor or midwife.
