# Mga check-up / Check-ups

Magpa-check-up nang hindi bababa sa walong beses habang buntis. Ang una ay bago mag-12 linggo, at ang mga susunod ay sa ika-20, 26, 30, 34, 36, 38 at 40 linggo. WHO recommends at least eight check-ups (contacts) during pregnancy. The first is before 12 weeks, then at weeks 20, 26, 30, 34, 36, 38 and 40.
<!-- keywords: prenatal antenatal checkup consulta konsulta ilang beses how many often visits visit schedule iskedyul kailan when magpatingin magpacheck-up -->

Dalhin ang inyong Mother and Child Book o notebook ng check-up sa bawat konsulta, at isulat dito ang mga tanong para sa doktor o midwife. Bring your Mother and Child Book or check-up notebook to every visit, and write down your questions for the doctor or midwife.
<!-- keywords: bring dalhin dadalhin dala record libro booklet tanong questions ready ihanda -->

Sa check-up, susukatin ang inyong blood pressure at timbang at titingnan ang inyong ihi. Karaniwan ding ipinapagawa ang blood test para sa anemia at ang screening para sa HIV, syphilis at hepatitis B. At check-ups, your blood pressure and weight are measured and your urine is tested. You are also usually offered a blood test for anemia and screening for HIV, syphilis and hepatitis B.
<!-- keywords: laboratory lab laboratoryo tests test exam eksamen pagsusuri dugo urine urinalysis bp presyon screening -->

Inirerekomenda ng WHO ang isang ultrasound bago mag-24 linggo ng pagbubuntis. Nakakatulong ito para malaman kung ilang linggo na ang pagbubuntis at para matingnan ang sanggol. WHO recommends one ultrasound scan before 24 weeks of pregnancy. It helps confirm how many weeks pregnant you are and checks on the baby.
<!-- keywords: ultrasound ultra scan sonogram ultrasonic -->

Magpabakuna laban sa tetanus at diphtheria (Td) ayon sa payo ng health worker; ang bilang ng turok ay depende sa mga bakunang natanggap na ninyo noon. Get the tetanus-diphtheria (Td) vaccine as your health worker advises; how many doses you need depends on the vaccines you had before.
<!-- keywords: bakuna vaccine vaccination injection turok tetanus anti-tetano tt td immunization -->

Inumin araw-araw ang iron at folic acid na ibinigay ng health worker, ayon sa kanyang bilin. Nakakatulong ang mga ito na maiwasan ang anemia at tumutulong sa malusog na pagbubuntis. Take the iron and folic acid your health worker gives you every day, as instructed. They help prevent anemia and support a healthy pregnancy.
<!-- keywords: iron folic ferrous supplement supplements bitamina vitamins vitamin anemia anaemia kulang dugo -->

# Pagkain at inumin / Food and drink

Kumain ng iba't ibang pagkain araw-araw: kanin o tinapay, gulay, prutas, at isda, karne, itlog o munggo. Hindi kailangang kumain para sa dalawa. Eat a variety of foods every day: rice or bread, vegetables, fruit, and fish, meat, eggs or beans. You do not need to eat for two.
<!-- keywords: kain kakain kakainin pagkain food foods eat eating diet healthy masustansya nutrition nutrisyon meal meals ulam kanin dalawa -->

Normal ang pagdagdag ng timbang habang buntis. Kung gaano karami ang tama ay depende sa timbang mo bago nagbuntis, kaya itanong ito sa midwife. Huwag magdiyeta para pumayat habang buntis. Sa huling tatlong buwan lang kailangan ng dagdag na pagkain, mga 200 calories bawat araw. Gaining weight is a normal part of pregnancy. How much is right depends on your weight before pregnancy, so ask your midwife. Do not diet to lose weight while pregnant. You only need extra food in the last 3 months, about 200 calories a day.
<!-- keywords: timbang weight gain tumaba tataba itaba taba mataba pumayat payat diet diyeta calories kilo kilos bigat mabigat dagdag -->

Kumain ng prutas at gulay araw-araw, gaya ng malunggay, kangkong, saging, bayabas at dalandan. Eat fruit and vegetables every day, such as moringa (malunggay), water spinach (kangkong), bananas, guava and oranges.
<!-- keywords: prutas fruit fruits gulay vegetables veggies saging banana bayabas guava dalandan orange malunggay moringa kangkong maganda good best healthy kain kakain -->

Para sa dugo, kumain ng pagkaing mayaman sa iron: berdeng madahong gulay gaya ng malunggay at kangkong, munggo at iba pang beans, isda, karne, at itlog na luto nang mabuti. Iron-rich foods help prevent anemia: dark green leafy vegetables, beans and lentils, fish, meat, and well-cooked eggs.
<!-- keywords: anemia anaemia iron dugo kulang maputla pale blood kain kakain food foods -->

Ang prutas na may vitamin C, gaya ng bayabas, dalandan at kalamansi, ay tumutulong sa katawan na makuha ang iron mula sa pagkain. Iwasang uminom ng tsaa o kape kasabay ng pagkain. Fruit rich in vitamin C, such as guava, oranges and calamansi, helps your body absorb iron from food. Avoid drinking tea or coffee with meals.
<!-- keywords: vitamin iron absorb kalamansi calamansi bayabas guava tsaa tea kape coffee meals pagkain -->

Uminom ng sapat na malinis na tubig araw-araw. Kung hindi sigurado na malinis ang tubig, pakuluan muna ito. Drink enough clean water every day. If you are not sure the water is clean, boil it first.
<!-- keywords: tubig water inom iinom drink drinking hydration uhaw thirsty pakuluan boil -->

Limitahan ang caffeine sa mas mababa sa 200 mg bawat araw, na humigit-kumulang dalawang tasa ng instant na kape. May caffeine din ang tsaa, cola, energy drink at tsokolate. Keep caffeine to less than 200 mg a day, which is about two cups of instant coffee. Tea, cola, energy drinks and chocolate also contain caffeine.
<!-- keywords: kape coffee caffeine tsaa tea softdrinks coke cola energy drink tsokolate chocolate inom iinom uminom drink -->

Huwag uminom ng alak habang buntis, dahil walang napatunayang ligtas na dami ng alak para sa sanggol. Do not drink alcohol while pregnant, because no amount of alcohol is known to be safe for the baby.
<!-- keywords: alak alcohol beer serbesa inuman lasing wine gin tuba lambanog inom iinom uminom drink drinking -->

Huwag manigarilyo at umiwas sa usok ng sigarilyo ng ibang tao. Pinapataas ng paninigarilyo ang panganib ng pagkalaglag, maagang panganganak at maliit na sanggol. Do not smoke, and stay away from other people's cigarette smoke. Smoking raises the risk of miscarriage, early birth and a small baby.
<!-- keywords: sigarilyo yosi smoke smoking cigarette cigarettes usok manigarilyo naninigarilyo vape tobacco -->

Mga pagkaing dapat iwasan habang buntis: hilaw o hindi lutong karne, itlog at shellfish; atay; gatas na hindi pasteurized at malalambot na keso; pating, marlin at swordfish; at alak. Limitahan din ang kape at ang tuna. Foods to avoid in pregnancy: raw or undercooked meat, eggs and shellfish; liver; unpasteurized milk and soft cheeses; shark, marlin and swordfish; and alcohol. Also limit coffee and tuna.
<!-- keywords: iwasan iwasang iiwasan kainin kakainin bawal ipinagbabawal pagkain pagkaing foods food avoid list listahan eat -->

Iwasan ang hilaw o hindi lutong karne, itlog at shellfish. Lutuin ang karne hanggang wala nang pula o dugo, at lutuin ang itlog hanggang buo ang puti at pula. Ang hilaw na isda, gaya ng kinilaw, sushi o sashimi, ay ligtas lang kung na-freeze muna ang isda, kaya kung hindi sigurado, piliin ang lutong isda. Avoid raw or undercooked meat, eggs and shellfish. Cook meat until no pink or blood is left, and cook eggs until the white and yolk are solid. Raw fish, such as kinilaw, sushi or sashimi, is safe only if the fish was frozen first, so if you are not sure, choose cooked fish.
<!-- keywords: hilaw raw undercooked kinilaw kilawin sushi sashimi frozen freeze karne meat itlog egg eggs shellfish talaba tahong hipon isda fish avoid iwasan bawal kain kakain eat -->

Iwasan ang atay at mga produktong gawa sa atay, gaya ng liver spread, dahil masyadong mataas ang vitamin A nito para sa sanggol. Avoid liver and liver products such as liver spread, because they contain too much vitamin A for the baby.
<!-- keywords: atay liver spread pate vitamin avoid iwasan bawal kain kakain -->

Iwasan ang gatas na hindi pasteurized at ang malalambot na keso gaya ng brie at camembert, maliban kung niluto ito hanggang umuusok sa init. Avoid unpasteurized milk and soft cheeses such as brie and camembert, unless they are cooked until steaming hot.
<!-- keywords: gatas milk keso cheese pasteurized unpasteurised unpasteurized dairy avoid iwasan bawal -->

Iwasan ang pating, marlin at swordfish dahil mataas ang mercury ng mga ito. Huwag kumain ng higit sa apat na lata o dalawang steak ng tuna bawat linggo, at hindi hihigit sa dalawang serving ng oily fish bawat linggo. Avoid shark, marlin and swordfish because they are high in mercury. Eat no more than four cans or two steaks of tuna a week, and no more than two portions of oily fish a week.
<!-- keywords: isda fish tuna sardinas pating shark mercury marlin swordfish oily salmon avoid iwasan bawal kain kakain -->

Maghugas ng kamay gamit ang sabon at tubig bago kumain at pagkatapos humawak ng hilaw na karne, lupa o dumi ng pusa, at hugasan nang mabuti ang prutas at gulay. Wash your hands with soap and water before eating and after touching raw meat, soil or cat litter, and wash fruit and vegetables well.
<!-- keywords: hugas maghugas wash hands kamay sabon soap hygiene kalinisan malinis clean germs -->

Initin ang pagkain hanggang umuusok sa init sa buong bahagi nito, at ihiwalay ang hilaw na karne sa lutong pagkain. Reheat food until it is steaming hot all the way through, and keep raw meat away from cooked food.
<!-- keywords: tira leftovers leftover initin reheat ulam food safety hilaw raw luto cooked -->

Kung may alagang pusa, ipaubaya sa iba ang paglilinis ng dumi nito, o gumamit ng guwantes at maghugas ng kamay pagkatapos. Hindi kailangang ipamigay ang pusa. If you have a cat, let someone else clean the litter, or wear gloves and wash your hands afterwards. You do not need to give away your cat.
<!-- keywords: pusa cat cats litter dumi alaga pet pets toxoplasmosis -->

# Karaniwang nararamdaman / Common discomforts

Karaniwan ang pagduduwal at pagsusuka sa unang mga buwan, at kadalasang nawawala ito pagsapit ng ika-16 hanggang 20 linggo. Kumain nang kaunti pero madalas, pumili ng tuyong pagkain gaya ng crackers o tinapay, at patuloy na uminom ng tubig. Nausea and vomiting (morning sickness) are common in early pregnancy and usually ease by weeks 16 to 20. Eat small amounts often, choose plain dry foods such as crackers or toast, and keep drinking water.
<!-- keywords: suka nagsusuka pagsusuka duwal nasusuka hilo nahihilo nausea vomit vomiting morning sickness naglilihi paglilihi lihi sick -->

Maaaring makatulong ang luya, gaya ng salabat, para sa pagduduwal. Ginger, such as ginger tea (salabat), can help with nausea.
<!-- keywords: luya salabat ginger nausea suka nagsusuka duwal paglilihi lihi -->

Magpatingin agad kung hindi kayo makapagpanatili ng pagkain o tubig sa loob ng 24 oras, kung napakadilim ng inyong ihi, o kung hindi kayo umihi nang mahigit 8 oras. Get help urgently if you cannot keep food or water down for 24 hours, your urine is very dark, or you have not peed for more than 8 hours.
<!-- keywords: suka nagsusuka pagsusuka vomit vomiting dehydration ihi urine severe matindi tuloy-tuloy -->

Para sa heartburn o pag-asim ng sikmura, kumain nang kaunti pero madalas, huwag kumain sa loob ng 3 oras bago matulog, at umupo nang tuwid pagkatapos kumain. For heartburn, eat small meals often, avoid eating within 3 hours of bedtime, and sit upright after eating.
<!-- keywords: heartburn asim sikmura hyperacidity acid acidic indigestion kabag bloated impatso hapdi dibdib -->

Para sa hirap sa pagdumi, kumain ng maraming fiber mula sa gulay, prutas at whole grain, uminom ng maraming tubig, at maglakad o gumalaw araw-araw. For constipation, eat plenty of fiber from vegetables, fruit and whole grains, drink plenty of water, and stay active every day.
<!-- keywords: tibi constipation constipated dumi pagdumi dumumi poop matigas hirap -->

Para sa sakit ng likod, baluktutin ang tuhod at panatilihing tuwid ang likod kapag may pinupulot, iwasang magbuhat ng mabigat, at magsuot ng flat na sapatos. For back pain, bend your knees and keep your back straight when picking something up, avoid lifting heavy things, and wear flat shoes.
<!-- keywords: likod back pain backache balakang ngalay masakit sakit -->

Karaniwan ang kaunting pamamaga ng paa at bukung-bukong. Iwasang tumayo nang matagal, magsuot ng komportableng sapatos, at itaas ang paa kapag nagpapahinga. Some swelling of the feet and ankles is common. Avoid standing for long periods, wear comfortable shoes, and rest with your feet up.
<!-- keywords: manas namamanas maga namamaga pamamaga swelling swollen paa feet ankles bukung-bukong binti legs -->

Pumunta agad sa health facility kung biglang namaga ang mukha o kamay, lalo na kung may kasamang matinding sakit ng ulo o malabong paningin. Go to a health facility right away if your face or hands swell suddenly, especially with a severe headache or blurred vision.
<!-- keywords: manas namamanas maga namamaga mukha face kamay hands swelling sudden biglang pre-eclampsia preeclampsia -->

Kung madalas kayong pulikatin sa binti, sabihin ito sa health worker; may mga paraan para dito, kasama ang mga hindi gumagamit ng gamot. If you often get leg cramps, tell your health worker; there are ways to treat them, including options without medicine.
<!-- keywords: pulikat pinupulikat cramps cramp binti legs leg kalamnan -->

Karaniwan ang matinding pagod, lalo na sa unang 12 linggo. Magpahinga hangga't kaya, umupo nang nakataas ang paa, at tanggapin ang tulong ng pamilya. Feeling very tired is common, especially in the first 12 weeks. Rest as much as you can, sit with your feet up, and accept help from family.
<!-- keywords: pagod napapagod tired tiredness fatigue exhausted antok inaantok sleepy hina mahina -->

Maaaring mas madaling dumugo ang gilagid habang buntis dahil sa hormones, kaya magsipilyo nang maayos at magpatingin sa dentista. Gums can bleed more easily in pregnancy because of hormones, so brush well and see a dentist.
<!-- keywords: gilagid gums gum ngipin teeth tooth dentist dentista sipilyo brush -->

Karaniwan ang madalas na pag-ihi, pero kung mahapdi o masakit ang pag-ihi, magpatingin sa doktor o midwife sa araw ding iyon, at agad kung may lagnat o sakit sa likod o tagiliran. Needing to pee often is common, but if peeing hurts or burns, see a doctor or midwife the same day, and urgently if you also have a fever or pain in your back or side.
<!-- keywords: ihi umiihi pag-ihi wiwi pee peeing urine uti hapdi mahapdi burning impeksyon infection -->

# Galaw, pahinga at damdamin / Activity, rest and feelings

Mag-ehersisyo nang katamtaman nang hindi bababa sa 150 minuto bawat linggo, gaya ng mabilis na paglalakad, maliban kung iba ang payo ng health worker. Ang katamtaman ay bumibilis ang paghinga pero nakakapagsalita pa rin. Do at least 150 minutes of moderate activity a week, such as brisk walking, unless your health worker advises otherwise. Moderate means you breathe faster but can still talk.
<!-- keywords: ehersisyo exercise exercises lakad maglakad walking galaw activity workout sports zumba swimming langoy active yoga -->

Iwasan ang contact sports at mga gawaing madaling madapa o mahulog, at huwag humiga nang matagal nang nakatihaya pagkatapos ng 16 linggo. Avoid contact sports and activities where you could fall, and avoid lying flat on your back for long periods after 16 weeks.
<!-- keywords: ehersisyo exercise sports basketball volleyball contact avoid iwasan bawal higa humiga nakatihaya -->

Mula 28 linggo, matulog nang nakatagilid sa kaliwa o kanan, pati sa idlip sa araw. Kung magising kayong nakatihaya, tumagilid lang ulit. From 28 weeks, go to sleep on your side, left or right, including daytime naps. If you wake up on your back, just turn onto your side again.
<!-- keywords: tulog matulog sleep sleeping posisyon position tagilid nakatagilid nakatihaya higa humiga kama idlip nap -->

Ligtas ang pakikipagtalik habang buntis maliban kung sinabihan kayo ng doktor o midwife na huwag, at hindi ito nakakasakit sa sanggol. Sex is safe in pregnancy unless your doctor or midwife has told you not to, and it does not hurt the baby.
<!-- keywords: sex sekswal pakikipagtalik talik asawa partner intimacy -->

Karaniwan ang pag-aalala habang buntis. Pero kung madalas kayong malungkot o umiiyak, hindi makatulog, o nawalan ng gana sa mga dating gusto, sabihin ito sa midwife o doktor dahil may tulong para dito. Sa krisis, tumawag sa NCMH crisis hotline 1553, libre at bukas 24 oras. Worry is common in pregnancy. But if you often feel low or tearful, cannot sleep, or lose interest in things you used to enjoy, tell your midwife or doctor, because help is available. In a crisis, call the NCMH crisis hotline 1553, free and open 24 hours.
<!-- keywords: lungkot malungkot depression depressed anxiety anxious nag-aalala alala stress iyak umiiyak mental health takot kaba sad hotline ncmh -->

# Galaw ng sanggol / Baby's movements

Karaniwang nararamdaman ang galaw ng sanggol mula ika-16 hanggang 24 linggo. Kung wala pa kayong nararamdamang galaw pagsapit ng 24 linggo, magpatingin sa midwife. Most women feel their baby move between 16 and 24 weeks. If you have not felt any movement by 24 weeks, see your midwife.
<!-- keywords: galaw gumagalaw sipa sumisipa kick kicks kicking movement movements moving pitik baby sanggol kailan when -->

Kung humina, nag-iba o huminto ang galaw ng sanggol, magpatingin agad kahit gabi, at huwag nang hintayin ang susunod na araw. Hindi totoo na humihina ang galaw ng sanggol malapit sa panganganak. If your baby's movements slow down, change or stop, get checked straight away, even at night, and do not wait until the next day. It is not true that babies move less near the due date.
<!-- keywords: galaw gumagalaw sipa sumisipa kick kicks movement movements moving baby sanggol less humina mahina -->

# Mga babalang senyales / Danger signs

Pumunta agad sa ospital o health center kung may pagdurugo sa ari, kombulsyon, matinding sakit ng ulo na may malabong paningin, lagnat na may sobrang panghihina, matinding sakit ng tiyan, o hirap sa paghinga. Go to a hospital or health center straight away if you have vaginal bleeding, fits (convulsions), a severe headache with blurred vision, a fever and are too weak to get out of bed, severe abdominal pain, or fast or difficult breathing.
<!-- keywords: danger signs babala babalang senyales delikado emergency warning panganib ospital hospital agad -->

# Panganganak / Labor and birth

Mga senyales ng panganganak: paghilab na humahaba, lumalakas at dumadalas; paglabas ng malapot na mucus na may kaunting dugo (show); sakit ng likod; at pagputok ng panubigan. Signs of labor include contractions that get longer, stronger and closer together; a show (sticky mucus with a little blood); backache; and your waters breaking.
<!-- keywords: labor labour manganganak panganganak hilab humihilab contractions contraction sign signs senyales show due kabuwanan -->

Tumawag o pumunta sa inyong panganganakan kapag regular ang paghilab tuwing 5 minuto o mas madalas, kung pumutok ang panubigan, kung dinudugo, o kung humina ang galaw ng sanggol. Kung wala pang 37 linggo at sa tingin ninyo ay nanganganak na kayo, pumunta agad. Call or go to your birth facility when contractions come regularly every 5 minutes or more often, if your waters break, if you are bleeding, or if your baby is moving less. If you are less than 37 weeks pregnant and think labor has started, go straight away.
<!-- keywords: labor labour hilab contractions ospital hospital lying-in kailan when pupunta go punta manganganak panganganak 37 -->

Kung pumutok ang panubigan, gumamit ng pasador (hindi tampon) at tingnan ang kulay. Dapat itong malinaw; kung mabaho o iba ang kulay, pumunta agad sa panganganakan. If your waters break, use a pad (not a tampon) and look at the color. It should be clear; if it smells bad or is colored, go to your birth facility at once.
<!-- keywords: panubigan waters water bag broke breaking tubig pad pasador kulay colour color -->

Bago manganak, magplano: saang pasilidad manganganak at sinong sinanay na health worker ang tutulong, paano pupunta roon, ipong pera, at sino ang sasama. Gawin ito nang hindi bababa sa isang buwan bago ang due date. Before the birth, plan where you will give birth and which trained health worker will help, how you will get there, money set aside, and who will go with you. Do this at least a month before your due date.
<!-- keywords: plano plan birth plan maghanda paghahanda prepare preparation ospital hospital lying-in saan manganganak pera money transport sasakyan bag gamit -->

Mas ligtas manganak sa health facility kasama ang sinanay na health worker, para maalagaan kayo at ang sanggol bago, habang at pagkatapos manganak. It is safest to give birth in a health facility with a trained health worker, so you and your baby are cared for before, during and after the birth.
<!-- keywords: hilot home birth bahay manganak facility ospital hospital lying-in birthing center midwife safe ligtas saan -->

# Pagkatapos manganak / After the birth

Pagkatapos manganak, manatili sa pasilidad nang hindi bababa sa 24 oras, at magpatingin muli sa ika-2 hanggang 3 araw, sa ika-7 hanggang 14 na araw, at sa ika-6 na linggo. After giving birth, stay at the facility for at least 24 hours, then have check-ups at 2 to 3 days, at 7 to 14 days, and at 6 weeks.
<!-- keywords: postpartum postnatal pagkatapos after birth check-up checkup newborn sanggol bagong panganak -->

Pasusuhin ang sanggol sa loob ng unang oras pagkapanganak. Gatas ng ina lamang ang ibigay sa unang 6 na buwan, kahit tubig ay hindi, at ituloy ang pagpapasuso hanggang 2 taon o higit pa kasabay ng ibang pagkain. Start breastfeeding within the first hour after birth. Give only breast milk, not even water, for the first 6 months, then keep breastfeeding up to 2 years or beyond alongside other foods.
<!-- keywords: breastfeeding breastfeed pasuso pagpapasuso gatas breast milk dede formula bottle tubig water -->

# Due date / Due date

Ang due date ay tantiya lamang: 280 araw o 40 linggo mula sa unang araw ng huling regla. Your due date is an estimate: 280 days, or 40 weeks, from the first day of your last period.
<!-- keywords: due date edd kailan manganganak kabuwanan buwan weeks linggo ilang linggo regla period lmp -->
